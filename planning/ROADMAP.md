# NexusCRM Development Roadmap

## Phase 00 — Foundation

Repository setup, development environment, backend/frontend bootstrap,
Docker, PostgreSQL, quality tooling.

## Phase 01 — Authentication & RBAC

Authentication, users, roles, permissions, JWT, refresh tokens.

## Phase 02 — Customer Management

Customers, contacts, addresses, customer search and history.

## Phase 03 — Lead Management

Lead creation, assignment, qualification and conversion.

## Phase 04 — Opportunity Pipeline

Opportunity stages, pipeline management and history.

## Phase 05 — Quotes

Quote creation, quote items, pricing and approval.

## Phase 06 — Orders & Inventory

Order lifecycle, inventory reservation, transactions and concurrency.

## Phase 07 — Payments

Payments, payment records, refunds and idempotency.

## Phase 08 — Customer Support

Tickets, comments, assignments and ticket lifecycle.

## Phase 09 — Reporting

Sales reports, pipeline analytics and operational reporting.

## Phase 10 — Async Processing

RabbitMQ, notifications, email jobs, retries and dead-letter queues.

## Phase 11 — Production Hardening

Redis caching, performance testing, observability, security hardening
and deployment preparation.
